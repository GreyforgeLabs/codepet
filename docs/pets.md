## Regenerating

```
python3 tools/petart/extract.py --status            # which species have six final sheets
python3 tools/petart/extract.py --species owl       # extract one species, then re-index
python3 tools/petart/extract.py --all               # every ready species, shared images, index
python3 tools/petart/extract.py --check             # rebuild in a scratch dir and byte-compare
python3 tools/petart/extract.py --contact           # QA contact sheets (run directory)
CODEPET_ALLOW_PARTIAL_ART=1 python3 qml/tests/test_pet_assets.py
python3 qml/tests/test_pet_distinctness.py
```

Sources default to `~/Media/codepet-art/v0.2` (`--src` or `CODEPET_ART_SRC` to override). `*.reject-*.png` files are ignored; a species is ready only when all six sheets exist and match the sha256 values in its `provenance.json`. Fixes for individual cells (remap to another cell, horizontal flip, nudge, per-cell scale, fit exclusion, grounded hopper frame, source-sheet erase), per-sheet fixes (size scale, keying options, component reassignment) and species-wide keying options go in `tools/petart/overrides.json` with a reason; the reasons are copied into `PROVENANCE.md`.

## Limits

- Extraction quality was reviewed on contact sheets, not in the running overlay.
- **One shared timing table.** Issue #5 asks every type for its own key poses and timing, not the cat rig recoloured. v0.2 ships each species' own drawn poses (12 base and 12 care cells per stage), but every species plays them through the one A4 state table above: the same frame order, `frameMs` and mode, apart from the per-species frame lists noted there. Per-species pace exists only in locomotion (`pxPerTick`, pause ranges, hopper lift). Per-species idle and care timing and choreography (a slower capybara or turtle, quicker rabbit and frog beats) are deferred to a later pass that can review the motion in the running overlay.
- Sheet poses are generator output: frame-to-frame continuity of the locomotion cycles and care sequences is only as good as the sheets.
- Thin details thinner than one source pixel against the key (single whiskers) may be dropped by the despill and fringe erode.
- Floor poses are centred on the alpha centroid of their lowest 30 %; a pose too lopsided to centre that way (a leap, a long tail) is slid sideways just enough to stay inside the 2 px margin, so its feet may sit off-centre.
- The generator sometimes draws a stage's care sheet at a different character size than its base sheet; such sheets carry a measured `sheets` scale override (listed in `PROVENANCE.md`). Remaining size differences between poses come from the sheets.
- Hopper species (rabbit, frog, owl) keep the sheet's lift on locomotion frames; those frames are listed in `locomotion.airborneFrames` (and `states.locomotion.airborneFrames`) and exempt from the ground check.
- Aquatic habitats grow with the stage: `geometry.water.byStage` and `geometry.stageHitBoxes` give per-stage boxes; `geometry.water.outer` and `hitBox` are the union over all stages. Renderers should hit-test with the current stage's box.
- `geometry.pivot`, `propAnchor`, `badgeAnchor` and `water.swimBounds` (global and per stage) are recorded for later renderers; no v0.2 code reads them. `qml/tests/test_pet_assets.py` only checks that they are sane: the pivot on `groundY` for floor species, the anchors inside the canvas, and the swim bounds inside their water box and below its water line.
