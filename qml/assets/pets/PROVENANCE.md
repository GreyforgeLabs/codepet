## Summary

- **Generation route:** GPT Image 2 (OpenAI image generation) via the Codex CLI image tool on the project owner's ChatGPT plan, with each sheet anchored on the species' PR #7 source sheet (`docs/art-library/source/…`, merged in v0.2.0).
- **Licence:** Distributed under the repository MIT licence (`qml/assets/pets/LICENSE`) by Greyforge Labs. The frames are AI-generated with OpenAI image generation; no third-party asset, asset pack, download or trace is included. Copyright and distribution suitability have not been legally reviewed.
- **Shipped form:** 192×192 lossy WebP (quality 86, alpha lossless) per pose, extracted from 4×3 chroma-key sheets by `tools/petart/extract.py` (chroma key with despill and fringe erode, connected-component cell split, one scale per species and stage, stage factors 0.82 / 0.92 / 1.0, ground line y = 184). Extraction is deterministic; `extract.py --check` rebuilds and byte-compares.
- **Source sheets are not in Git** (about 200 MB). They are the release asset [`codepet-art-sources-v0.2.tar`](https://github.com/GreyforgeLabs/codepet/releases/download/v0.2.1/codepet-art-sources-v0.2.tar) of v0.2.1 (its sha256 is in that release's `SHA256SUMS`); every sheet's sha256 is listed below and in each `manifest.json` (`source.sheets`). Reproduction steps: `docs/pets.md`.
- **PR #7 library:** the 15 PR #7 sheets in `docs/art-library/` are the style anchors the v0.2 sheets were generated from (each species' reference hash is listed below). No PR #7 pixels are shipped directly.
- **Shared images:** `_placeholder.webp` and `_memorial.webp` are drawn with Pillow by `tools/petart/shared_art.py` (original, MIT).
- **Legacy art:** the ginger SVGs in `qml/assets/` keep their own `qml/assets/PROVENANCE.md`.
